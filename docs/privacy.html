PeekTodo Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026. Applies to the Android app PeekTodo (package com.chloeyeo.peektodo), published by Chloe Yeo.

PeekTodo is a to-do list that shows your open tasks in a notification on your lock screen. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: your tasks stay on your phone, and the app does not collect, transmit or share anything.

Data the app stores

  • Your to-do items: the text you type, whether each item is done, and when it was created and last edited.
  • Two settings: whether blur mode is on and whether the notification is pinned.

All of this is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. PeekTodo has no account system, and you never provide a name, email address or any other personal information to use it.

Data the app does not collect

PeekTodo does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking libraries, and no third-party SDKs that process user data. The developer never receives your tasks or any information about how you use the app.

Sharing

PeekTodo does not share data with anyone, because it never has access to it outside your device. There are no third parties to disclose.

The lock screen notification

The app posts one notification containing your open tasks. Android shows notifications on the lock screen according to your system settings, which means your task text can be visible to anyone who can see your locked phone. You control this in two ways: turn on blur mode in the app so the lock screen shows only the number of open tasks, or use your phone's own lock screen settings to hide notification content. Unlocking the phone shows the full list again.

Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to show your to-do list as a notification. Without it the app still works as a plain list.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): to put the notification back after your phone restarts.

No other permissions are requested. The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or device identifiers.

Device backups

If you have Android's backup feature enabled, Android may include PeekTodo's local data in the backup of your device to your Google account, alongside other apps' data. That backup is performed by Android and Google under their own terms, not by PeekTodo, and you can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

Deleting your data

Delete individual tasks inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored. Because nothing is kept anywhere else, there is nothing further to delete and nothing to request from the developer.

Children

PeekTodo is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes what it does with data, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Continued use after an update means the updated policy applies.
